Check off the bins and your partner's phone knows.

Did you get the milk? Now you both just know. Alex checks off the bins and Jamie's phone buzzes. Jamie's dentist appointment shows up on Alex's calendar. Sleep, steps and to-dos add up to a winner every Sunday. And when you miss each other, there's a button for a hug.

All in the app.

Shared to-dos

  • Type it the way you'd say it.
    "Bins every Sunday 7pm"
  • Repeats every few days, weeks or months, or a set time after it's done.
  • Your partner gets a notification when you check one off. Undo in time and they never see it.

Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. Miso asks for read-only access to your calendars and uses it only to show your events in the app to you and your partner. It never creates, changes or deletes events. Google data is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone outside your household. Miso's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
